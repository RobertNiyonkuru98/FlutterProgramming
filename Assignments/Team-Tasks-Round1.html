🩺 What we know for certain

Confirmed by Robert, who works in pharmacy systems. Nobody designs anything before reading this.

  1. Almost every pharmacy in Rwanda accepts cash. Some also accept specific insurance providers. No pharmacy is insurance-only — cash always works.
  2. A prescription comes from a hospital or clinic, is signed and stamped, and is tied to your insurance card.
  3. You take the prescription and your insurance card to a pharmacy that accepts that specific insurer.
  4. You pay your share per whatever percentage your insurance covers.
  5. Each pharmacy displays which insurances it accepts on a board or sticker at the door. That is the only public record of it — physical, and only readable if you are standing in front of that pharmacy.
  6. There is no app, no website, no directory to search "which pharmacies near me accept my insurer."

💡 Our product in one sentence

We digitise the board on the pharmacy door.

The information already exists and is already public. Today you can only read it by walking there — one pharmacy at a time. That is the gap. It is the whole reason our app should exist.
