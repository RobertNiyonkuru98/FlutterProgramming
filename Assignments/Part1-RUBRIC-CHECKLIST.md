## B. The five rubric-only catches

These are graded but are **missing from the submission-criteria list** you were given. Teams lose these every time.

| # | Catch | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sensory notes (Sights, Sounds, Feels, Smells)** in empathy-map quadrants | Empathy Maps (5 pts) |
| 2 | **An Introduction & Mission Alignment section** — not in the submission list at all | 3 pts |
| 3 | **A reframed problem statement from the user's perspective** — separate from your original problem statement | UVP row (5 pts) |
| 4 | **The recruitment plan + recruitment email kept as evidence** — the email itself is graded | 5 pts |
| 5 | **Persona photo + environment + a real interview quote** | Personas (5 pts) |

Two more that are easy to under-deliver:
- **Figma needs 10+ screens and IA needs 8+ screens** — count them before you freeze design.
- **Storyboard needs 6–10 panels** — worth 5 points, and it's the cheapest 5 points in the whole assignment.

## C. Claims → Evidence audit (make it in 15 minutes)

The rubric's heaviest row (10 pts) says *"insight traceable to participants"*. Prove it in one table in the PDF:

| Insight | Which participants (P#) | Verbatim quote | Where it shows up in design |
|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. members don't trust digital balances | P02, P05, P09 | *"…we count the cash three times."* | Home screen shows live group balance + audit log |
| | | | |

Fill this **after synthesis, before design freeze**. It simultaneously defends:
- Interview Execution, Ethics & Evidence (traceability)
- Empathy Maps (direct quotes)
- Journey Map (opportunities → features)
- Figma Prototype (screens map to research)
- UVP (reframed problem from user's perspective)
- Reflection section

One table, six rubric rows. That is the highest-leverage 15 minutes in this project.
